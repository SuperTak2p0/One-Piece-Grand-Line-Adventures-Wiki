Factions

Released

Align with the Marines, join a Pirate Crew, or work for the World Government — your choice.

About this Feature

With the faction system, you can choose your own path in the world of ONE PIECE. Your faction decides your goals, allies, and enemies. Available Factions:

  • Pirate
    Become a pirate and start your journey to become the Pirate King. The World Government and the Marines will become your enemies. You can join an existing crew or even create your own crew.
  • Marine
    Fight against pirates and protect justice. Work together with other Marines or fight alone to rise through the Marine ranks.
  • Bounty Hunter
    Hunt down pirates and collect bounties to earn money and become rich.
  • Revolutionary
    Join the Revolutionary Army and fight against the World Government.

Current State

Right now, only the faction selection system is available. In future releases, full faction mechanics, features, and progression systems will be added.

How to Join a Faction

Option 1 (only first time)

When you first join the world, the faction selection screen will appear. You can choose between the available factions. If you click the faction button, the next faction will be selected.

Option 2 (any time)

Use this command (press T and type):

/onepiece setfaction faction.onepiecemod.[faction]

Replace [faction] with your desired faction (pirate, marine, bountyhunter, revolutionary). And to check your faction, use:

/onepiece getfaction

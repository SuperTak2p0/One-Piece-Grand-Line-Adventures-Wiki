Haki System

Coming Soon

Train your Haki abilities — Observation, Armament, and Conqueror's — to overpower any foe.

About this Feature

In the update 1.4 the basic System of Haki will get added with Buso- and Kenbunshoku (Armament- and Observation-Haki).
You can achieve Haki with the training alone or with Trainer (from update 2.0).
You can expect Haoshoku (Conqueror Haki) from update 3.4 - 4.0

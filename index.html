⚔️ Features

🍎

Devil Fruits

Discover and consume powerful Devil Fruits granting extraordinary abilities at the cost of swimming.

⛵

Custom Ships

Build and sail your own ship across vast oceans. Recruit a crew and explore the world.

🗡️

Haki System

Train your Haki abilities — Observation, Armament, and Conqueror's — to overpower any foe.

🏝️

New Biomes

Explore Grand Line islands, sky islands, and underwater kingdoms with unique mobs and loot.

🤝

Factions

Align with the Marines, join a Pirate Crew, or work for the World Government — your choice.

💰

Bounty System

Your bounty rises with every victory. Higher bounties attract stronger Marine bounty hunters.

🚀 Getting Started

1

Install a Mod Loader

Download and install Forge or Fabric for your Minecraft version. The mod page on CurseForge lists the required version.

2

Download the Mod

Grab the latest release from CurseForge and drop the .jar file into your mods/ folder.

3

Launch Minecraft

Open the Minecraft Launcher, select the Forge/Fabric profile, and hit Play. The mod will load automatically.

4

Create or Join a World

Start a new world or connect to a server with the mod installed. You will spawn near the ocean — your adventure begins!

5

Find a Devil Fruit

Search chests in dungeons and villages for Devil Fruits. Right-click to consume one and unlock your powers.
